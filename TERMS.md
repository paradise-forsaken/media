# Paradise Forsaken Publisher - Terms of Service

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Paradise Forsaken Publisher is an internal tool used by the developer of the game Paradise Forsaken to upload the game's own promotional video clips to the game's own social media accounts.

## Who may use it

The tool is not offered to the public. It is used only by the Paradise Forsaken developer, with accounts the developer owns.

## What it does

- Uploads video clips of Paradise Forsaken to the developer's own accounts.
- On TikTok, clips are sent to the account's inbox as drafts; the account owner reviews and publishes each one in the TikTok app.

## Content

All uploaded content is created and owned by the Paradise Forsaken developer and must follow the terms and community guidelines of the platform it is posted to.

The tool is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.

# Paradise Forsaken Publisher - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Paradise Forsaken Publisher is an internal tool used by the developer of the game Paradise Forsaken to upload the game's own promotional video clips to the game's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the developer.

## Data the tool accesses

When the developer connects one of their own accounts, the tool receives:

- Basic profile information for that account (such as its display name and account identifier), used only to confirm the right account is connected.
- An access token and refresh token, used only to upload the developer's own video clips.

## How the data is stored

Tokens are stored locally on the developer's own computer. They are not shared with, sold to, or sent to any third party.

## Data the tool does not collect

The tool does not collect, store, or process data about any other person. It does not read followers, messages, comments, or any other account's content.

## Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by revoking the app in the account's settings on the relevant platform, which invalidates the stored tokens.

## Contact

Questions can be raised by opening an issue on this repository.
